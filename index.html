Projects

Netflix customer analytics: churn and retention

Problem: Which customers cancel, and what separates them from those who stay?

Finding: Basic subscribers churn at 25.2%, more than 3 times the Premium rate (7.6%). Cancelled customers also watch less (227 vs 262 minutes per week).

Action: Target Basic subscribers with retention offers and use low weekly watch time as an early-warning signal.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • SQL
  • Power BI

UK house prices, 1968 to 2025

Problem: How have UK house prices changed over time, and how do they differ by region, property type and buyer?

Finding: The average UK price rose 8,062%, from £3,311 in 1968 to £270,259 in 2025. Detached homes cost 2.3 times as much as flats, and former owner-occupiers pay about 45% more than first-time buyers in England.

Built: A cleaned dataset of 149,085 rows, SQL analysis with window functions in DuckDB, and a four-page Power BI dashboard on a star schema with DAX measures.

  • Python
  • SQL (DuckDB)
  • Power BI
  • DAX
